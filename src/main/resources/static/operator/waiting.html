Waiting Priority Queue

Waiting Queue · PriorityQueue · O(log n)

Ordered by Priority (EMERGENCY > HIGH > NORMAL) and arrival timestamp FIFO. Automatically reallocated upon vehicle exit.

Smart Reallocation Invariant: Custom Priority Comparator

When an occupied slot is released, the engine inspects the queue and extracts the highest-priority compatible waiting vehicle (incompatible vehicles are buffered and re-inserted, preserving priority ordering).
